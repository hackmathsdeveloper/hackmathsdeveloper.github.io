## Section 1: Divisibility and Prime Factorization (Problems 1–30)

1. Prove that $1001$ divides the $201$-digit integer $1000\dots001$ consisting of two $1$s separated by $200$ zeros.
2. Let $m > n \ge 0$. Prove that $(2^{2^n} + 1)$ divides $(2^{2^m} - 1)$.
3. Show that a positive integer $n$ is divisible by $9$ if and only if the sum of its decimal digits $S(n)$ is divisible by $9$.
4. Prove that for any positive integer $n$, the fraction $\frac{21n + 4}{14n + 3}$ is irreducible.
5. Prove that $\gcd(n! + 1, (n+1)! + 1) = 1$ for all positive integers $n$.
6. Prove that there exist infinitely many positive integers that cannot be expressed as the sum of two power numbers (where a power number has all prime exponent powers $\ge 2$).
7. Show that there are infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that $n \mid (2^n + 1)$.
8. Let $a, b, c, d$ be positive integers. Prove that $240 \mid (a^{4b+d} - a^{4c+d})$.
9. Let $a, b, c$ be integers such that $a + b + c = 0$. Prove that $d = a^{1999} + b^{1999} + c^{1999}$ is not prime.
10. Prove that no number of the form $11\dots11$ (a sequence of $n \ge 2$ ones) is a perfect square.
11. Show that no $7$-digit number formed using each of the digits $1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7$ exactly once is a multiple of another such number.
12. Let $f(x) = x^3 + ax^2 + bx + c$ with $a, b, c \in \mathbb{Z}$. Prove that there exist infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that $f(n)$ is not a perfect square.
13. Find all positive integers $n$ such that $n \cdot 2^n + 1$ is a perfect square.
14. Prove that $n^4 + 4^n$ is composite for all integers $n > 1$.
15. Show that for any integer $n \ge 1$, $n(n+1)(n+2)(n+3)$ cannot be a perfect square.
16. Find all prime numbers $p$ such that $2^p + p^2$ is also prime.
17. Prove that for any positive integer $k$, there exist $k$ consecutive composite positive integers.
18. Find all integer solutions to $x^2 + y^2 = 2025$.
19. Prove that $p^2 - 1$ is divisible by $24$ for any prime $p > 3$.
20. Show that if $a, b$ are positive integers such that $ab + 1$ divides $a^2 + b^2$, then $\frac{a^2 + b^2}{ab + 1}$ is a perfect square.
21. Prove that $3^n + 1$ is not divisible by $2^n$ for any $n > 2$.
22. Let $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}^+$. Prove that if $a^2 + b^2$ is divisible by $7$, then both $a$ and $b$ are divisible by $7$.
23. Find all positive integers $n$ such that $3^n - 1$ is a power of $2$.
24. Prove that $n^5 - n$ is divisible by $30$ for every integer $n$.
25. Find all primes $p$ such that $8p^2 + 1$ is also prime.
26. Show that $2^{2024} - 1$ has at least $10$ distinct prime factors.
27. Prove that $n^3 + 2n$ is always divisible by $3$ for any integer $n$.
28. Find all pairs of integers $(x, y)$ such that $xy - 3x - 2y = 0$.
29. Determine all positive integers $n$ such that $2^n - 1$ is divisible by $7$.
30. Show that if $2^n - 1$ is prime, then $n$ must be prime.

## Section 2: Modular Arithmetic and Congruences (Problems 31–70)

31. Prove that for any integer $x$, $x^2 \equiv 0, 1, 4 \pmod 8$.
32. Find the last two digits of $7^{2026}$.
33. Solve the congruence $5x \equiv 3 \pmod{13}$.
34. Find the remainder when $2^{100}$ is divided by $11$.
35. Show that $1^n + 2^n + 3^n + 4^n$ is divisible by $5$ if and only if $4 \nmid n$.
36. Determine the remainder of $19^{2025}$ when divided by $7$.
37. Prove that $a^2 + b^2 \equiv 3 \pmod 4$ has no integer solutions.
38. Find all integer solutions to $x^3 \equiv 2 \pmod 7$.
39. Prove that for any prime $p$, $(p-1)! \equiv -1 \pmod p$.
40. Calculate $3^{1000} \pmod{100}$.
41. Find the remainder of $\sum_{k=1}^{100} k!$ when divided by $12$.
42. Solve $x^2 \equiv 1 \pmod{35}$.
43. Prove that $13 \mid (4^{2n+1} + 3^{n+2})$ for all $n \ge 0$.
44. Find the system solution: $x \equiv 2 \pmod 3$, $x \equiv 3 \pmod 5$, $x \equiv 2 \pmod 7$.
45. Prove that $7 \mid (a^2 + b^2) \implies 7 \mid a$ and $7 \mid b$.
46. Find all integers $n$ such that $n^2 + 1$ is divisible by $n + 1$.
47. Determine $2^{100} \pmod{100}$.
48. Show that $x^4 + y^4 = 1000003$ has no integer solutions.
49. Prove that $11 \mid (30^{99} + 61^{99})$.
50. Find the smallest positive integer $x$ satisfying $7x \equiv 1 \pmod{31}$.
51. Find all $n$ such that $1! + 2! + \dots + n!$ is a perfect square.
52. Show that $3^{2n+2} + 8n - 9$ is divisible by $64$ for all $n \ge 1$.
53. Prove that no integer of the form $8k+7$ can be written as the sum of three squares.
54. Calculate $17^{17^{17}} \pmod{10}$.
55. Prove that $5^n + 2 \cdot 3^{n-1} + 1$ is divisible by $8$ for all odd $n$.
56. Find the remainder when $2025^{2026}$ is divided by $9$.
57. Prove that $x^2 - 5y^2 = 2$ has no integer solutions.
58. Find all $n \in \mathbb{Z}^+$ such that $n \mid (n-1)! + 1$.
59. Solve $12x \equiv 9 \pmod{15}$.
60. Show that $a^4 + b^4 + c^4 \neq d^4 + 3$ for any integers $a, b, c, d$.
61. Find the remainder of $2^{2026}$ when divided by $13$.
62. Show that $n^7 - n$ is always divisible by $42$.
63. Find all integer solutions to $3^x - 2^y = 1$.
64. Determine whether $x^2 \equiv 5 \pmod{23}$ has a solution.
65. Show that $10^n \equiv 1 \pmod 9$ for all positive integers $n$.
66. Prove that $6 \mid (n^3 - n)$ for every integer $n$.
67. Find the last three digits of $5^{2026}$.
68. Show that if $p$ is an odd prime, $1^{p-1} + 2^{p-1} + \dots + (p-1)^{p-1} \equiv -1 \pmod p$.
69. Prove that $x^2 + y^2 = 3z^2$ has no non-zero integer solutions.
70. Compute $2^{3^4} \pmod{11}$.

## Section 3: GCD, LCM, and Bezout's Identity (Problems 71–100)

71. Find $\gcd(2a + 13b, 3a + 5b)$ if $\gcd(a, b) = 1$.
72. Prove that $\gcd(a^n - 1, a^m - 1) = a^{\gcd(n,m)} - 1$ for any integer $a > 1$.
73. Let $a, b$ be coprime integers. Prove that $\gcd(a+b, a-b)$ is either $1$ or $2$.
74. Find all pairs $(a, b)$ of positive integers such that $\gcd(a,b) + \operatorname{lcm}(a,b) = a + b$.
75. Prove that $\gcd(n! + 1, (n+1)!) = 1$.
76. Show that for any $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}^+$, $ab = \gcd(a,b) \cdot \operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$.
77. Find $\gcd(F_n, F_{n+1})$ where $F_n$ is the $n$-th Fibonacci number.
78. Prove that if $\gcd(a, m) = 1$ and $\gcd(b, m) = 1$, then $\gcd(ab, m) = 1$.
79. Find all integers $x, y$ such that $15x + 21y = 6$.
80. Determine $\gcd(2^{2024} - 1, 2^{2026} - 1)$.
81. Show that $\gcd(a^2 + b^2, a+b)$ divides $2\gcd(a,b)^2$.
82. Prove that $\frac{\operatorname{lcm}(a,b,c)^2}{\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)\operatorname{lcm}(b,c)\operatorname{lcm}(c,a)} = \frac{\gcd(a,b,c)^2}{\gcd(a,b)\gcd(b,c)\gcd(c,a)}$.
83. Solve $17x + 13y = 1$ in integers.
84. Show that if $\gcd(a, b) = 1$, then $\gcd(a+b, ab) = 1$.
85. Find all $n$ such that $\gcd(n, 100) = 10$.
86. Prove that $\gcd(3^n - 1, 3^m + 1)$ is at most $2$.
87. Prove that for any positive integers $a$ and $b$, $\gcd(a, b) = \gcd(a, b - a)$.
88. Find the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $\operatorname{lcm}(n, 40) = 280$.
89. Prove that if $a \mid bc$ and $\gcd(a,b) = 1$, then $a \mid c$.
90. Find all solutions to $\gcd(a, b) = 12$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(a, b) = 180$.
91. Show that $\gcd(2^n - 1, 2^n + 1) = 1$ for all $n \ge 1$.
92. Prove that $\gcd(a^2, b^2) = \gcd(a,b)^2$.
93. Find $\gcd(n^3 + 1, n^2 + 1)$ for integer $n$.
94. Determine all $a, b$ such that $a + b = 56$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(a, b) = 105$.
95. Prove that $\gcd(a, b) = \gcd(a-b, b)$.
96. Find the number of ordered pairs $(a, b)$ with $\operatorname{lcm}(a, b) = 1000$.
97. Show that $\gcd(n, n+2)$ is $1$ if $n$ is odd and $2$ if $n$ is even.
98. Find all integers $x, y$ such that $35x + 55y = 10$.
99. Prove that $\gcd(a+b, \operatorname{lcm}(a,b)) = \gcd(a,b)$.
100. Calculate $\gcd(2^{100} - 1, 2^{120} - 1)$.

## Section 5: Euler's Totient, Fermat's Little Theorem, and Wilson's Theorem (Problems 141–170)

141. Compute $\phi(3600)$ where $\phi$ is Euler's totient function.
142. Prove that $a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod p$ for any prime $p \nmid a$.
143. Calculate $7^{82} \pmod{100}$ using Euler's Totient Theorem.
144. Show that $\phi(n)$ is even for all $n > 2$.
145. Prove Wilson's Theorem: $(p-1)! \equiv -1 \pmod p$ for prime $p$.
146. Find all positive integers $n$ such that $\phi(n) = 12$.
147. Prove that $\sum_{d \mid n} \phi(d) = n$ for every positive integer $n$.
148. Calculate $2^{1000} \pmod{13}$ using Fermat's Little Theorem.
149. Show that $n \mid \phi(a^n - 1)$ for any $a > 1, n > 1$.
150. Solve $\phi(n) = n/2$.
151. Prove that if $p$ is prime, $(p-2)! \equiv 1 \pmod p$.
152. Calculate $5^{2026} \pmod{18}$ using Euler's Totient Theorem.
153. Find all $n$ such that $\phi(n) = \phi(2n)$.
154. Prove that $a^{\phi(b)} + b^{\phi(a)} \equiv 1 \pmod{ab}$ if $\gcd(a, b) = 1$.
155. Compute $\phi(101)$.
156. Show that for any composite $n > 4$, $(n-1)! \equiv 0 \pmod n$.
157. Find all integers $n$ such that $\phi(n) \mid n$.
158. Prove that $17 \mid (2^{3n+1} + 3^{2n}+1)$ is not always true, but evaluate $2^{16} \pmod{17}$.
159. Compute $\phi(p^k)$ for a prime $p$ and integer $k \ge 1$.
160. Calculate $3^{100} \pmod{7}$ using Fermat's Little Theorem.
161. Show that $n^p - n$ is divisible by $p$ for any prime $p$ and integer $n$.
162. Find all $n$ such that $\phi(n) = 8$.
163. Prove that $p \mid ((p-1)! + 1)$ implies $p$ is prime.
164. Compute $12^{100} \pmod{35}$ using Euler's Totient Theorem.
165. Find the sum of all positive integers less than $n$ and coprime to $n$.
166. Prove that $\phi(ab) = \phi(a)\phi(b)$ if and only if $\gcd(a, b) = 1$.
167. Show that $7 \mid (2^{30} - 1)$.
168. Find the smallest $n$ such that $\phi(n) \ge 100$.
169. Prove that $p^2 \mid (a^p - a)$ is not generally true, but determine when $p^2 \mid (a^{p-1} - 1)$ (Wieferich primes).

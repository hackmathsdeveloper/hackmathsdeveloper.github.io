
The internet has a surprisingly rich ecosystem of Mathematical Tripos material: official Cambridge past papers and syllabi, large collections of lecture notes and example sheets, plus several curated “Tripos-style” resource sites and historical archives. [neelnanda](https://www.neelnanda.io/tripos)

## Official Cambridge materials

These are the canonical sources tied directly to the current Tripos.

- Undergraduate maths course overview (structure of IA/IB/II, links out to many resources): the Faculty’s **Undergraduate Mathematics: Course Information** page. [maths.cam.ac](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/course)
- Past Tripos examination papers:
  - Part IA, IB, II, and III past papers are available as PDFs from the Faculty site. [maths.cam.ac](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/pastpapers/maths-ia/2018)
  - Individual year pages list all papers (e.g., Mathematical Tripos Part IA examination papers 2018 with Paper 1–4 PDFs). [maths.cam.ac](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/pastpapers/maths-ia/2018)
  - Part III 2018 page shows the full list of advanced courses and their exam papers (functional analysis, algebraic geometry, PDEs, QFT, etc.). [maths.cam.ac](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/postgrad/part-iii/current/tripos-examination-papers/2018)
- Tripos-specific resource hub: Cambridge’s **Tripos-specific resources** page aggregates:
  - DPMMS and DAMTP example sheets.
  - Past exam papers.
  - Schedules, lecture lists, course descriptions (IA, IB, II).
  - Part III example sheets and course/essay descriptions.
  - “Study Skills in Mathematics” booklet and exam-prep advice. [maths.cam.ac](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/studentreps/tripos-specific-resources)

These official pages give you the full exam corpus and high-level structure but (deliberately) not official solutions. [maths.cam.ac](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/studentreps/tripos-specific-resources)

## Unofficial lecture notes and sheets

A lot of the real value comes from semi-official/unofficial notes aggregated by faculty and students.

- Tripos-specific resources page – lecture notes section:
  - Lists many lecture notes by course, author, and year across IA, IB, II, and III.
  - Includes classic notes by Tom Körner, David Tong, Richard Weber, etc., for courses like Analysis I/II, Vector Calculus, Probability, Numerical Analysis, Metric & Topological Spaces, Representation Theory and more. [maths.cam.ac](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/studentreps/tripos-specific-resources)
  - Notes are often PDFs, sometimes course websites, and cover both current and historical variants of courses. [maths.cam.ac](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/studentreps/tripos-specific-resources)
- Dexter Chua’s notes:
  - Linked from the Tripos resources page as a major unofficial source for Parts IA–II. [maths.cam.ac](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/studentreps/tripos-specific-resources)
  - Contains comprehensive typed lecture notes for most undergraduate courses; these are widely used by students.  
- Gareth Taylor’s “maths: tripos papers” site:
  - Re-organises Maths Tripos past papers by course rather than by year. [tartarus](https://tartarus.org/gareth/maths/tripos/)
  - Helpful if you want to drill a single course (e.g., Groups, Rings and Modules) across multiple years.  

These collections are what you’d typically mine if you want to self-study a course to Tripos level using more than just exam papers. [tartarus](https://tartarus.org/gareth/maths/tripos/)

## Curated Tripos-style resources

There are some modern curated collections explicitly aimed at people “trying the Tripos” outside Cambridge.

- “Try The Mathematical Tripos”:
  - A site assembling detailed syllabi, archived notes, example sheets, computational assignments, and exams so that outsiders can “try it ourselves.” [learnaifromscratch.github](https://learnaifromscratch.github.io/math.html)
  - It collates material from the official Cambridge sites and unofficial notes into a single accessible study path. [learnaifromscratch.github](https://learnaifromscratch.github.io/math.html)
- Neel Nanda’s Tripos resources:
  - A collection of intuition-focused notes built while studying the Maths Tripos. [neelnanda](https://www.neelnanda.io/tripos)
  - Includes:
    - Second year: notes for Complex Analysis, Linear Algebra, Groups/Rings/Modules, Analysis II, Metric & Topological Spaces (merged now into Analysis & Topology), Quantum Mechanics, Numerical Analysis, Geometry, Variational Principles, Statistics, Markov Chains, etc. [neelnanda](https://www.neelnanda.io/tripos)
    - Third year: notes for Linear Analysis (functional analysis), Galois Theory, Graph Theory, Number Fields, Probability & Measure, Logic and Set Theory, plus rougher notes for Algebraic Topology, Analysis of Functions, Machine Learning, Representation Theory, etc. [neelnanda](https://www.neelnanda.io/tripos)
  - Also includes Anki flashcards targeting definitions, theorem statements, proof sketches, intuitions, motivations, and exam technique. [neelnanda](https://www.neelnanda.io/tripos)

For a self-study programme, these curated sites are usually more efficient than navigating raw faculty pages yourself. [learnaifromscratch.github](https://learnaifromscratch.github.io/math.html)

## Question archives and historical Tripos papers

If you want breadth, history, or to benchmark difficulty, there are dedicated archives.

- Mathematics Tripos Questions site:
  - An archive of questions from Cambridge Mathematics Tripos exams. [questions.tripos](https://questions.tripos.org)
  - Designed for browsing and practicing Tripos questions outside the original PDFs.  
- Historical Tripos papers in public-domain form:
  - Example: **“Papers set in the Mathematical Tripos: in the University of Cambridge, 1913–1917”** on Internet Archive. [archive](https://archive.org/details/papersetinmathtr00cambrich)
  - Gives you full scanned books of papers from that era; these can be freely downloaded and studied.  

These are useful if you’re interested in the evolution of difficulty/content or want unusual problem sets. [archive](https://archive.org/details/papersetinmathtr00cambrich)

## Syllabi, schedules, and guides

To organise study or map Tripos content to your existing background, these are handy.

- Schedules and syllabi:
  - The Faculty hosts a **Schedules (pdf)** document listing course syllabi and learning outcomes. [maths.cam.ac](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/files/schedules.pdf)
  - Part III guide to courses (e.g., 2018–2019 PDF) lists all graduate-level lecture courses with brief descriptions and exam information. [maths.cam.ac](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/postgrad/part-iii/files/misc/part-iii-guide-to-courses.pdf)
- Course descriptions and lecture lists:
  - Linked on the undergraduate course page for Parts IA, IB, II. [maths.cam.ac](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/course)

Between these and the curated resources, you can reconstruct a fairly faithful version of the Tripos curriculum for self-study. [maths.cam.ac](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/files/schedules.pdf)

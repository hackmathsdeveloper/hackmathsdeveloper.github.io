
Here are 50 distinct types or themes of combinatorial geometry problems (framed as problem families rather than specific exercises):

1. Extremal point–line incidences (e.g., Szemerédi–Trotter–type questions: max number of incidences between n points and m lines). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
2. Incidences between points and higher‑dimensional hyperplanes (generalizations of Szemerédi–Trotter to \(\mathbb{R}^d\)). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
3. Erdős distinct distances problem in the plane (min number of distinct distances among n points). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
4. Unit distance problems (max number of unit‑length segments determined by n planar points). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
5. Repeated distance problems (max multiplicity of a given distance among n points). [gilkalai.wordpress](https://gilkalai.wordpress.com/2013/07/06/some-old-and-new-problems-in-combinatorics-and-geometry/)
6. Point–circle incidence problems (bounds on incidences between points and circles).  
7. Incidences with algebraic curves (e.g., pseudo‑lines, pseudo‑circles, curves of bounded degree). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
8. Helly‑type intersection problems for convex sets (conditions on subfamilies implying global intersection). [artofproblemsolving](https://artofproblemsolving.com/community/c1285h1012490_combinatorial_geometry)
9. Fractional Helly and (p, q)‑theorems for families of convex sets (intersection guarantees under density assumptions). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
10. Transversal (piercing) number vs. packing number for convex set families (minimum hitting set vs. maximum disjoint subfamily). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)

11. Covering problems with translates or homothets of a convex body (min number of copies to cover a region). [sciencedirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/chapter/edited-volume/abs/pii/B9780720422627500296)
12. Packing problems with congruent or similar convex bodies (max number of pairwise disjoint copies in a region). [sciencedirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/chapter/edited-volume/abs/pii/B9780720422627500296)
13. Kissing number–type problems (max number of equal spheres touching a central one in \(\mathbb{R}^d\)). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kissing_number)
14. Sphere packing density problems in various dimensions (e.g., densest packings in \(\mathbb{R}^d\)). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
15. Circle packing in the plane under combinatorial constraints (e.g., specified tangency graph).  
16. Covering the plane with unit disks (min density, multiple coverings, coverings with constraints). [sciencedirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/chapter/edited-volume/abs/pii/B9780720422627500296)
17. Problems on plank coverings and Tarski–Bang plank theorems (covering convex bodies with strips). [sciencedirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/chapter/edited-volume/abs/pii/B9780720422627500296)
18. Kakeya‑type geometric set problems (minimal measure sets containing a unit segment in every direction).  
19. Illumination problems for convex bodies (min number of directions or points needed to “illuminate” a convex body). [sciencedirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/chapter/edited-volume/abs/pii/B9780720422627500296)
20. Problems about widths, diameters, and directional measures of convex bodies (e.g., extremal ratios). [sciencedirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/chapter/edited-volume/abs/pii/B9780720422627500296)

21. Combinatorics of convex polytopes (face numbers, f‑vectors, h‑vectors, upper and lower bound theorems). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
22. Realization problems for graphs as 1‑skeleta of convex polytopes (Steinitz‑type questions). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
23. Neighborly polytopes and cyclic polytopes (maximizing the number of faces for fixed dimension and vertices).  
24. Counting or bounding the number of k‑faces in arrangements of hyperplanes or segments (Zaslavsky‑type results). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
25. Arrangements of lines in the plane: number of cells, faces, and combinatorial types for n lines. [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
26. Incidence combinatorics in hyperplane arrangements (zones, levels, k‑sets). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
27. Problems on pseudoline arrangements (combinatorial types, allowable sequences).  
28. Geometric permutations and order types of point sets (realization types under orientation tests).  
29. Ham‑sandwich and centerpoint‑type partitioning problems (fair division using hyperplanes). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
30. Equipartition by hyperplanes (e.g., Grünbaum’s problems, splitting multiple measures equally). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)

31. Erdős–Szekeres “happy ending” problems (largest convex polygon in a planar point set). [users.mccme](https://users.mccme.ru/mskopenkov/skopenkov-pdf/mcl29.pdf)
32. No‑three‑in‑line and general position problems (max size of sets avoiding certain collinearities). [users.mccme](https://users.mccme.ru/mskopenkov/skopenkov-pdf/mcl29.pdf)
33. Problems on crossings in geometric graphs (crossing number, thrackle conjecture variants). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
34. Geometric Ramsey‑type problems (monochromatic configurations under edge/point colorings).  
35. Minimum or maximum number of convex holes (empty convex polygons) in point sets. [users.mccme](https://users.mccme.ru/mskopenkov/skopenkov-pdf/mcl29.pdf)
36. Visibility and stabbing problems in simple polygons or arrangements (e.g., fewest guards, art gallery variants in discrete settings).  
37. Delaunay triangulations and Voronoi diagrams: extremal properties (e.g., max degree, number of regions).  
38. Geometric spanner problems (graph on points with bounded stretch and bounded number of edges).  
39. Unit disk graphs and intersection graphs of geometric objects: chromatic, clique, and independence numbers. [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
40. Problems on geometric hypergraphs (e.g., hyperedges given by points inside a range such as half‑planes or disks). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)

41. Set‑system discrepancy problems for geometric ranges (half‑spaces, boxes, disks). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
42. Epsilon‑nets and epsilon‑approximations for geometric range spaces (size bounds, constructions). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
43. VC‑dimension calculations for geometric set systems (e.g., half‑spaces, boxes, simplices). [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
44. Problems on Minkowski sums of finite point sets or convex polytopes (face structure and combinatorial complexity).  
45. Sum‑product and distance‑set problems in \(\mathbb{R}^d\) linking combinatorial geometry with additive combinatorics. [ipam.ucla](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/workshops/workshop-ii-combinatorial-geometry/)
46. Incidences over finite fields and combinatorial geometry in \(\mathbb{F}_q^d\) (finite field Kakeya, distance problems).  
47. Problems on geometric matchings (e.g., noncrossing perfect matchings, maximizing or minimizing total length).  
48. Geometric TSP variants over point sets with combinatorial constraints (e.g., noncrossing tours).  
49. Partitioning sets by convex hulls or half‑spaces (e.g., Radon, Tverberg and their variants). [users.mccme](https://users.mccme.ru/mskopenkov/skopenkov-pdf/mcl29.pdf)
50. Topological methods in combinatorial geometry (Borsuk–Ulam‑type arguments in Helly/Tverberg/centerpoint results). [sciencedirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/chapter/edited-volume/abs/pii/B9780720422627500296)


Here are **30 variations** of the given geometric setup (three mutually tangent congruent circles enclosed by an outer triangle, or variations involving their centers, radii, contact points, and enclosing figures).

---

### **Category 1: Numerical & Ratio Problems (Standard to Intermediate)**

1. **Outer Triangle Side Length:**
Three identical circles of radius $r = 1$ are externally tangent to each other. An equilateral triangle $OPQ$ circumscribes all three circles such that each side of the triangle is tangent to two circles. Find the side length of $\triangle OPQ$.
*Answer:* $2 + 2\sqrt{3}$
2. **Area of the Enclosed Central Gap:**
Three identical circles of radius $r$ are externally tangent to each other at points including $E$. Find the area of the curvilinear triangular region bounded between the three circles in terms of $r$.
*Answer:* $r^2 \left(\sqrt{3} - \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$
3. **Area Ratio (Inner Triangle to Outer Triangle):**
Let $A, B, C$ be the centers of the three mutually tangent congruent circles, forming $\triangle ABC$. Let $\triangle OPQ$ be the outer equilateral triangle circumscribing the three circles. Find the ratio of the area of $\triangle ABC$ to the area of $\triangle OPQ$.
*Answer:* $\frac{1}{(1 + \sqrt{3})^2} = 2\sqrt{3} - 3$
4. **Outer Triangle Height:**
If the radius of each of the three mutually tangent circles is $r = 6$, calculate the height (altitude) of the outer equilateral triangle $\triangle OPQ$.
*Answer:* $6(3 + \sqrt{3})$
5. **Distance to Outer Vertex:**
Let $C$ be the center of the top circle, and $O$ be the top vertex of the circumscribing equilateral triangle $\triangle OPQ$. If each circle has radius $r$, find the length of the segment $OC$.
*Answer:* $r(\sqrt{3} + 1)$ (or $r/\sin 30^\circ + r = 2r$ from center to vertex along altitude, yielding $OC = r\sqrt{3}$).
6. **Inscribed Central Circle Radius:**
A small circle centered at $D$ is tangent to all three congruent circles of radius $r$ from the outside (in the central gap). Express the radius of this small central circle in terms of $r$.
*Answer:* $r \left(\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3} - 1\right)$
7. **Circumscribed Outer Circle Radius:**
Find the radius $R$ of a circle centered at $D$ that circumscribes all three mutually tangent circles of radius $r$.
*Answer:* $r \left(1 + \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\right)$
8. **Tangent Segment Length ($OF$):**
Let $F$ be the point of tangency of the top-left side of $\triangle OPQ$ with the bottom-left circle centered at $A$. If $r = 3$, find the length of segment $OF$.
*Answer:* $3 + 3\sqrt{3}$
9. **Perimeter Ratio:**
Find the ratio of the perimeter of $\triangle ABC$ (formed by the circle centers) to the perimeter of $\triangle OPQ$.
*Answer:* $\frac{1}{1 + \sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{3} - 1}{2}$
10. **Area Outside Circles but Inside $\triangle OPQ$:**
If $r = 2$, calculate the area inside the outer triangle $\triangle OPQ$ that is NOT covered by any of the three circles.
*Answer:* $4(3 + 2\sqrt{3}) - 6\pi$

### **Category 2: Proof & Conceptual Problems**

11. **Concentricity Proof:**
Prove that the centroid of $\triangle ABC$ (formed by the circle centers) coincides with the centroid of the outer circumscribing triangle $\triangle OPQ$.
12. **Homothety / Dilatation:**
Show that $\triangle OPQ$ is a dilation of $\triangle ABC$ from their common center $D$. Determine the scale factor of this dilation in terms of $r$.
*Answer:* Scale factor $k = 1 + \sqrt{3}$
13. **Parallel Sides Proof:**
Prove that side $AB$ of the center triangle is parallel to side $PQ$ of the outer triangle.
14. **Tangential Contact Properties:**
Let $E$ be the point of tangency between the circles centered at $A$ and $B$. Prove that $E$ is the midpoint of segment $AB$ and that line $CE$ is an altitude of $\triangle ABC$.
15. **Perpendicularity of Tangent Lines:**
Prove that the altitude of $\triangle OPQ$ passing through vertex $O$ passes through point $C$, point $D$, and the point of tangency $E$ between the two bottom circles.
16. **Collinearity of Centroid, Center, and Vertex:**
Prove that the vertex $O$, the center $C$ of the upper circle, the central point $D$, and the midpoint $E$ of side $AB$ are all collinear.
17. **Cyclic Quadrilateral:**
If $F$ is the tangency point on $OP$ and $E$ is the contact point between bottom circles, prove that points $A, F, O, E$ form a cyclic quadrilateral or lie on a specific locus.

### **Category 3: Advanced Geometry & Generalizations**

18. **Unequal Radii Variation:**
Suppose two bottom circles have radius $r_1 = 1$ and the top circle has radius $r_2 = 2$. If they are mutually tangent, find the side lengths of $\triangle ABC$ formed by their centers.
*Answer:* Sides are $2, 3, 3$ (an isosceles triangle).
19. **Inradius of Center Triangle:**
For three identical circles of radius $r$, calculate the inradius of $\triangle ABC$.
*Answer:* $\frac{r}{\sqrt{3}}$
20. **Circumradius of Center Triangle:**
Calculate the circumradius of $\triangle ABC$ formed by the centers of the three unit circles.
*Answer:* $\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}$
21. **Circle Tangent to Outer Triangle & Central Gap:**
A fourth circle is placed at $O$ inside the corner angle of $\triangle OPQ$, tangent to $OP, OQ$, and externally tangent to the top circle centered at $C$. Find its radius $r_4$ in terms of $r$.
*Answer:* $r_4 = \frac{r}{3}$
22. **Inscribed Square in Gap:**
Determine the maximum side length of a square that can be placed inside the central region bounded by the three tangent circles of radius $r$.
23. **Locus of Vertex $O$:**
If the two bottom circles remain fixed and tangent to each other while the top circle rolls along their outer boundaries staying tangent to both, describe the locus of vertex $O$ of the circumscribing triangle.
24. **Coordinate Geometry Challenge:**
Place the origin at point $E$ (tangency of bottom two circles). Find the exact Cartesian coordinates of points $A, B, C, D,$ and vertices $O, P, Q$ in terms of $r$.
*Answer:* $A(-r,0), B(r,0), C(0, r\sqrt{3}), D\left(0, \frac{r\sqrt{3}}{3}\right), O\left(0, r(\sqrt{3} + 2)\right)$, etc.
25. **Angle Bisector Property:**
Prove that line $AO$ bisects $\angle FAE$, where $F$ is the tangency point on side $OP$ and $E$ is the contact point between the two lower circles.
26. **Packing Efficiency:**
Calculate the ratio of the total area of the three circles to the area of the outer triangle $\triangle OPQ$ (the packing density of this configuration).
*Answer:* $\frac{3\pi r^2}{(2+\sqrt{3})\sqrt{3} r^2} = \frac{\pi\sqrt{3}}{2 + \sqrt{3}} \approx 81.7\%$
27. **Perpendicular Bisector Intersection:**
Show that the perpendicular bisectors of the sides of $\triangle OPQ$ intersect at point $D$, the center of symmetry of the three circles.
28. **Excenter Relation:**
Show that $C$ is the incenter of $\triangle OPQ$'s top region cut off by the common tangent line to the top circle parallel to $PQ$.
29. **Extension to 3D (Spheres in a Tetrahedron):**
Four congruent spheres of radius $r$ are mutually tangent to each other and enclosed inside a regular tetrahedron. Find the side length of the tetrahedron in terms of $r$.
*Answer:* $2r\left(1 + \sqrt{6}\right)$
30. **Recursive Packing (Apollonian Gasket Variant):**
If a smaller circle $C_1$ is inscribed in the central gap between the three circles of radius $r=1$, and then three even smaller circles $C_2$ are inscribed in the new gaps created between $C_1$ and each pair of original circles, find the radius of $C_2$.

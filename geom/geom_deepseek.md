
Here are 10 elementary geometry problems involving triangles and circles.

---

**1. The Inscribed Angle**
A circle has a center $O$. Points $A$, $B$, and $C$ lie on the circle's circumference. If the central angle $\angle AOB$ is $80^\circ$, what is the measure of the inscribed angle $\angle ACB$?

**2. The Right Triangle in a Semicircle**
A triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in a semicircle with diameter $AB$. If the length of the diameter $AB$ is $10$ cm and the side $AC$ is $6$ cm, what is the length of the side $BC$?

**3. The Circumference of an Equilateral Triangle**
An equilateral triangle with a side length of $12$ cm is inscribed in a circle. What is the radius of the circle?

**4. Tangent and Radius**
A circle with center $O$ has a radius of $5$ cm. A line is tangent to the circle at point $T$. A point $P$ lies on this tangent line, $12$ cm away from point $T$. What is the length of the segment $OP$?

**5. Isosceles Triangle in a Circle**
An isosceles triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in a circle, where $AB = AC$. If the vertex angle $\angle BAC$ is $40^\circ$, what is the measure of the angle $\angle ABC$?

**6. Area of a Triangle**
A triangle has a base of $10$ inches. Its height is $7$ inches. What is the area of the triangle?

**7. Circle Inscribed in a Square**
A circle is inscribed in a square with a side length of $8$ cm. What is the area of the circle?

**8. Angles in a Triangle**
In triangle $PQR$, the measure of angle $P$ is $50^\circ$ and the measure of angle $Q$ is $60^\circ$. What is the measure of angle $R$?

**9. Chord Length**
In a circle with a radius of $13$ cm, a chord is drawn $5$ cm from the center of the circle. What is the length of the chord?

**10. Exterior Angle of a Triangle**
In triangle $XYZ$, an exterior angle at vertex $Z$ is $120^\circ$. If the measure of the interior angle at vertex $X$ is $55^\circ$, what is the measure of the interior angle at vertex $Y$?

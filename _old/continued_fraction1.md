## Functional equation

\[
C(x)=\frac{1}{1+\frac{x}{1+\frac{x^2}{1+\cdots}}}.
\]

From above, the tail after the first denominator is again the same pattern, so the whole expression satisfies  
\[
C(x)=\frac{1}{1+xC(x)}.
\]
That is because the part under the \(x\) is again \(C(x)\). ## Closed form

Solve the quadratic for \(C(x)\):
\[
C(x)=\frac{-1\pm \sqrt{1+4x}}{2x}.
\]
The correct branch is the one with \(C(0)=1\), so
\[
C(x)=\frac{-1+\sqrt{1+4x}}{2x}.
\]
This follows directly from the continued fraction’s self-similarity and the requirement that the power series starts with constant term 1. Write
\[
C(x)=\sum_{n\ge 0} a_n x^n.
\]
Then from
\[
C(x)=\frac{1}{1+xC(x)}
\]
or equivalently
\[
xC(x)^2+C(x)-1=0,
\]
you get the recurrence
\[
a_0=1,\qquad a_n=-\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} a_k a_{n-1-k}\quad(n\ge 1).

## Coefficient formula

Using the standard Catalan closed form,
\[
\mathrm{Cat}_n=\frac{1}{n+1}\binom{2n}{n},
\]
the coefficients here are
\[
a_n=(-1)^n\frac{1}{n+1}\binom{2n}{n}.
\]
So one identity derived from the continued fraction is
\[
\frac{-1+\sqrt{1+4x}}{2x}
=\sum_{n\ge 0}(-1)^n\frac{1}{n+1}\binom{2n}{n}x^n.
\]

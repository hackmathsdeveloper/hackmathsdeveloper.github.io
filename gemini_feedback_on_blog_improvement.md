### 2. Core Operational Observations

1. **The "Mathematics" Super-Category Overcrowding:**
Over 50% of the articles carry the top-level `Mathematics` category alongside subcategories (e.g., `/galois theory/mathematics/...`). Having a catch-all category for a math blog dilutes taxonomy usefulness and weakens search indexing/navigation.
2. **Under-Represented Core Identity ("AI & Hacking"):**
Despite the tagline *"Mathematical Hacking with AI"*, **Machine Learning** accounts for only 2 posts (~1.6%), and there are no dedicated categories for **AI Reasoning**, **Computational Mathematics**, or **Symbolic AI**.
3. **High Concentration in Pure Math Pillars:**
The site's strongest content density lies in:
* **Analytic & Special Functions** (Special Functions + Analysis = 18 posts)
* **Algebraic Structures** (Galois Theory + Group Theory + Elliptic Curves + Algebra = 20 posts)
* **Number Theory** (12 posts)

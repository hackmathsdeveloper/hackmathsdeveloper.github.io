
Yes — here are 30 different **examples** of elliptic functions, built from standard families such as the Weierstrass \(\wp\)-function and the Jacobi elliptic functions. An elliptic function is a meromorphic function that is doubly periodic, and standard examples include \(\wp(z)\), its derivative \(\wp'(z)\), and rational combinations of Jacobi functions like \(\operatorname{sn}(z,k)\), \(\operatorname{cn}(z,k)\), and \(\operatorname{dn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_function)

## Weierstrass examples

Fix a lattice \(\Lambda\subset\mathbb{C}\), and let \(\wp(z)=\wp(z;\Lambda)\) be the associated Weierstrass elliptic function. The derivative \(\wp'(z)\) is also elliptic with the same lattice, and any rational function of \(\wp(z)\) and \(\wp'(z)\) is again elliptic. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_function)

Here are 10 examples:
1. \(\wp(z)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_function)
2. \(\wp'(z)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_function)
3. \(\wp(z)^2\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_function)
4. \(\wp(z)^3\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_function)
5. \(\dfrac{1}{\wp(z)-a}\), where \(a\) is a constant not making the function identically singular. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_function)
6. \(\dfrac{\wp'(z)}{\wp(z)-a}\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_function)
7. \(\dfrac{\wp'(z)^2}{\wp(z)-a}\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_function)
8. \(\wp(z)+\wp'(z)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_function)
9. \(\dfrac{\wp(z)^2+1}{\wp'(z)}\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_function)
10. \(\dfrac{\wp'(z)^2+\wp(z)}{\wp(z)^2-1}\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_function)

## Jacobi examples

Fix a modulus \(k\), and consider the Jacobi elliptic functions \(\operatorname{sn}(z,k)\), \(\operatorname{cn}(z,k)\), and \(\operatorname{dn}(z,k)\). These are standard elliptic functions, and the classical secondary Jacobi functions are formed by taking reciprocals and quotients of these basic ones. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)

Here are 12 examples:
11. \(\operatorname{sn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
12. \(\operatorname{cn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
13. \(\operatorname{dn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
14. \(\operatorname{ns}(z,k)=1/\operatorname{sn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
15. \(\operatorname{nc}(z,k)=1/\operatorname{cn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
16. \(\operatorname{nd}(z,k)=1/\operatorname{dn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
17. \(\operatorname{sc}(z,k)=\operatorname{sn}(z,k)/\operatorname{cn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
18. \(\operatorname{sd}(z,k)=\operatorname{sn}(z,k)/\operatorname{dn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
19. \(\operatorname{cd}(z,k)=\operatorname{cn}(z,k)/\operatorname{dn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
20. \(\operatorname{cs}(z,k)=\operatorname{cn}(z,k)/\operatorname{sn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
21. \(\operatorname{ds}(z,k)=\operatorname{dn}(z,k)/\operatorname{sn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
22. \(\operatorname{dc}(z,k)=\operatorname{dn}(z,k)/\operatorname{cn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)

## More constructed examples

Because sums, products, and quotients of elliptic functions are elliptic whenever they remain meromorphic, many further examples can be constructed from the basic Jacobi functions. This gives an easy way to build many distinct elliptic functions without leaving the standard theory. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_function)

Here are 8 more:
23. \(\operatorname{sn}(z,k)^2\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
24. \(\operatorname{cn}(z,k)^2\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
25. \(\operatorname{dn}(z,k)^2\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
26. \(\operatorname{sn}(z,k)\operatorname{cn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
27. \(\operatorname{sn}(z,k)\operatorname{dn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
28. \(\operatorname{cn}(z,k)\operatorname{dn}(z,k)\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
29. \(\dfrac{\operatorname{sn}(z,k)^2}{\operatorname{dn}(z,k)}\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)
30. \(\dfrac{\operatorname{cn}(z,k)+\operatorname{dn}(z,k)}{\operatorname{sn}(z,k)}\). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)

## Small note

If you want these to be fully rigorous as a list of elliptic functions, it is understood that we choose parameters so denominators do not vanish identically; isolated poles are allowed because elliptic functions are meromorphic, not necessarily entire. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_function)

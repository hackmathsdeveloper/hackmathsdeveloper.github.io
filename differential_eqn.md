
**Here are 50 different types of differential equations**, spanning major classifications and well-known named equations:

### Basic Classifications
1. **Ordinary Differential Equation (ODE)** – involves derivatives with respect to a single independent variable  
2. **Partial Differential Equation (PDE)** – involves partial derivatives with respect to two or more independent variables  
3. **Linear Differential Equation** – the unknown function and its derivatives appear to the first power only, with no products between them  
4. **Nonlinear Differential Equation** – contains powers, products, or other nonlinear functions of the unknown function or its derivatives  
5. **Homogeneous Differential Equation** – every term contains the dependent variable or its derivatives (or, for first-order, of the form \(f(y/x)\))  
6. **Nonhomogeneous (Inhomogeneous) Differential Equation** – has a nonzero forcing/source term  
7. **First-Order Differential Equation** – highest derivative is of order 1  
8. **Second-Order Differential Equation** – highest derivative is of order 2  
9. **Higher-Order Differential Equation** – order ≥ 3  
10. **System of Differential Equations** – multiple equations involving several unknown functions  

### First-Order ODE Types
11. **Separable Differential Equation** – can be written as \(g(y)\,dy = f(x)\,dx\)  
12. **Exact Differential Equation** – of the form \(M(x,y)\,dx + N(x,y)\,dy = 0\) where \(\partial M/\partial y = \partial N/\partial x\)  
13. **Linear First-Order ODE** – \(y' + P(x)y = Q(x)\)  
14. **Bernoulli Equation** – \(y' + P(x)y = Q(x)y^n\) (\(n \neq 0,1\))  
15. **Riccati Equation** – \(y' = P(x) + Q(x)y + R(x)y^2\)  
16. **Clairaut’s Equation** – \(y = xy' + f(y')\)  
17. **Autonomous Equation** – right-hand side depends only on the dependent variable (\(y' = f(y)\))  
18. **Homogeneous First-Order ODE** – \(y' = f(y/x)\)  

### Important Named ODEs
19. **Abel Equation**  
20. **Airy Equation** – \(y'' - xy = 0\)  
21. **Bessel Equation** – \(x^2 y'' + x y' + (x^2 - \nu^2)y = 0\)  
22. **Modified Bessel Equation**  
23. **Cauchy–Euler (Equidimensional) Equation** – \(a_n x^n y^{(n)} + \cdots + a_0 y = 0\)  
24. **Chebyshev Equation**  
25. **Legendre Equation**  
26. **Associated Legendre Equation**  
27. **Laguerre Equation**  
28. **Associated Laguerre Equation**  
29. **Hermite Equation**  
30. **Hypergeometric Equation**  
31. **Confluent Hypergeometric (Kummer) Equation**  
32. **Mathieu Equation**  
33. **Hill Equation**  
34. **Duffing Equation** – nonlinear oscillator with cubic term  
35. **van der Pol Equation** – nonlinear oscillator with limit cycle  
36. **Painlevé Equations** (a family of six nonlinear second-order ODEs)  
37. **Emden–Fowler Equation**  
38. **Ermakov (Yermakov) Equation**  
39. **Heun Equation**  

### Important PDEs
40. **Heat (Diffusion) Equation** – \(u_t = k u_{xx}\)  
41. **Wave Equation** – \(u_{tt} = c^2 u_{xx}\)  
42. **Laplace’s Equation** – \(\nabla^2 u = 0\)  
43. **Poisson’s Equation** – \(\nabla^2 u = f\)  
44. **Transport (Advection) Equation** – \(u_t + c u_x = 0\)  
45. **Burgers’ Equation** – nonlinear advection-diffusion equation  
46. **Korteweg–de Vries (KdV) Equation** – nonlinear dispersive wave equation  
47. **Sine-Gordon Equation**  
48. **Schrödinger Equation** (time-dependent or time-independent)  
49. **Klein–Gordon Equation**  
50. **Navier–Stokes Equations** – system of nonlinear PDEs for fluid motion  

These cover the main structural classifications used in textbooks as well as many of the classic named equations that appear in mathematics, physics, and engineering. Many equations can belong to several categories at once (e.g., the Bessel equation is a second-order linear homogeneous ODE).
